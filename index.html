Imposter!

Everyone gets the secret word… except the imposter.

Quick rules

  1. One person taps Host the game. Their phone or tablet runs the game, and they play too.
  2. Everyone else scans the QR code, types their name and taps Join.
  3. Everyone sees the category. Everyone except the imposter sees the secret word.
  4. Go around the circle twice. Each player says one clue word per turn, so that's 2 clue words each.
  5. Vote for who you think the imposter is. Pick 2 names if you think there are 2 imposters. Then the host taps Big reveal!

📖 How to play

The idea

Everyone gets the same secret word… except the imposter, who only knows the category. Give clues that show you know the word, without making it too easy. Then vote: who's faking it?

What you need

  • 3 to 12 players, all in the same room.
  • One phone or tablet each, with internet.
  • About 5 minutes a round.

Set up

  1. One person hosts. They tap Host the game, type their name and tap Create game. The host plays too.
  2. A QR code and a 4-letter code appear on the host's screen.
  3. Everyone else scans the QR code with their phone camera (or opens this game, taps Join as a player and types the code), types their name and taps Join.
  4. The host picks a category (or 🎲 Surprise me) and taps Start game when everyone's names are showing.

Playing a round

  1. Look at your card in secret. Tap Tap to see your card, read it, then tap Hide my card. Don't let anyone see!
  2. Most people see the secret word. The imposter sees "You're the imposter!". With 4 or more players there might be 2 imposters.
  3. The host taps Spin to choose who goes first (it's never an imposter).
  4. Go around the circle twice. On your turn say one clue word. That's 2 clue words each.
  5. Then the host taps Choose imposter. Everyone votes on their own phone. You can change your vote until the reveal.
  6. The host taps Big reveal. Your phone shows if you were right!

Tips

  • Not too easy, not too hard. For "Pizza", "cheese" is too easy; "triangle" is just right.
  • Imposter: listen carefully, then say something that fits the others' clues. Act confident!
  • Don't say the secret word, or a word that gives it away straight off.

If something goes wrong

  • "Can't find game": check the 4-letter code on the host's screen.
  • A phone drops out: open the game again; it reconnects by itself.
  • Wrong person joined: the host taps their name in the lobby to remove them.
  • The host's phone must stay on this game until you finish. If it closes, the game ends.
